What this website is like

These four principles come from co-creation sessions with people with low vision and blindness, families and professionals.

  • {{icon:a11y}} Accessible to everyone

    Works with screen readers and braille displays, has a button to listen to every page and lets you choose a dark or light background, text size and spacing.

  • {{icon:network}} Three areas, two clicks

    Patients; family and community; professionals. Plus an assistant that takes you to the content you need.

  • {{icon:heart}} Realistic and caring

    Neither “nothing more can be done” nor “you can do anything”. We explain what changes, what can be done and how to adapt daily life.

  • {{icon:qr}} Designed for the clinic

    Professionals can tick resources and hand them to patients as an accessible PDF with a QR code.

Why this platform?

Information on low vision exists, but it is scattered. In the co-creation sessions, some people explained that it took them years to learn that optical aids or rehabilitation existed. Low Vision Network aims to shorten that path.

  • 1.46% of the European population has low vision World Health Organization estimate
  • ≈ 116,800 people with low vision in Catalonia Estimate based on WHO and Idescat data
  • 9 people with low vision or blindness took part in the November 2026 co-creation sessions Co-creation sessions report
  • 40,000+ people seen through the Mirades Solidàries programme since 2007 University Vision Centre (CUV-UPC)

Who is behind it

An alliance between the University Vision Centre (CUV-UPC), Associació Discapacitat Visual Catalunya: B1+B2+B3 and Fundació Punt de Vista.
