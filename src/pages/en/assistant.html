Explain in your own words what is happening or what you are looking for. The assistant suggests up to three pieces of content from this website, reviewed by the project team.

{{assistant}}

How it works

  1. Who you are: a patient, family or someone interested, or a professional. If you do not say, the assistant works it out from what you type.
  2. What you need: whether you have just been diagnosed, want to understand a condition or symptom, or are looking for practical solutions.
  3. Answer: a short message and at most three links to verified content.

The assistant only uses content from this website. It does not make up answers or give diagnoses. If it finds nothing suitable, it says so and suggests where to get help. What you type does not leave your browser; if you use the microphone, dictation is handled by your browser.

You can also browse the three areas directly: {{nav:patients}}, {{nav:families}} and {{nav:professionals}}.
