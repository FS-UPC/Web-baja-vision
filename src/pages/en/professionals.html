Resources for professionals

For ophthalmologists, optometrists, primary care doctors and social workers. The goal: no patient should leave the clinic without knowing where to go next.

{{icon:bulb}} Three key ideas

  1. Refer early. Vision rehabilitation should not be reserved for the most severe cases: the sooner, the better the adaptation.
  2. Communicate with empathy. Avoid “nothing more can be done” and always offer a concrete next step.
  3. Hand over resources. With the generator, you can prepare an accessible PDF with a QR code for your patient in one minute.
