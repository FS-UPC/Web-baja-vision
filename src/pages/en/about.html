{{icon:users}} About us

An alliance for low vision

Clinical and academic knowledge, the lived experience of people with visual impairment and accessibility expertise, working together. The aim is not to reinvent existing resources, but to bring them together, validate them and make them more human.

The organisations

  • {{logo_tile:upc}}

    University Vision Centre (CUV-UPC)

    Coordinator. University health centre of the Faculty of Optics and Optometry of Terrassa (UPC), with a specialist low vision unit. Principal investigator: Prof. Lluís Pérez Mañá.

    CUV website

  • {{logo_tile:b1b2b3}}

    Associació Discapacitat Visual Catalunya: B1+B2+B3

    Partner. Defends the rights and inclusion of people with visual impairment and contributes their direct experience.

    Association website

  • {{logo_tile:puntdevista}}

    Fundació Punt de Vista

    Partner. Accessibility, inclusive design and content adaptation for people with visual impairment.

    Foundation website

  • {{logo_tile:coooc}}

    Official College of Opticians and Optometrists of Catalonia (COOOC)

    Collaborator. The professional body for optics and optometry in Catalonia. It hosted the November 2026 co-creation sessions at its headquarters.

    COOOC website
