{{icon:family}} Area 2 · Family and community

Family, friends, schools and society

For relatives, friends, partners, carers, teachers and anyone who wants to understand low vision and help without taking away independence.

{{icon:bulb}} The most important things

  1. Ask before helping. The person knows better than anyone what they need.
  2. Do not judge by what you see. Someone may read their phone and still walk into a door, or go without a cane by day and need it at night.
  3. Be clear and specific. “The cup is on your right” is better than “it's over there”.
  4. Do not overprotect. Doing everything for the other person is not always helping.
  5. Look after yourself. Supporting someone is tiring too. Ask for support when you need it.
