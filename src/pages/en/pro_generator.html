How it works

  1. Tick the resources. The preview updates instantly.
  2. Press Create the PDF. The browser's print window will open: choose Save as PDF to get the file, or print it directly.
  3. Give it to the patient or send them the file. The QR code opens the same resources online, where they can enlarge text, change contrast or listen.

Accessibility: the PDF keeps the heading and list structure (current browsers produce tagged PDFs), using the Atkinson Hyperlegible typeface at a large size.

Privacy: the generator runs in your browser. No patient data is sent or stored.
