Take part in user testing

Before the website opens to everyone, it is being tested by the people who took part in the co-creation sessions and anyone else who wishes. Your feedback decides what needs to change.

What we want to know

  • Whether it works well with your screen reader (VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA, JAWS) or braille display.
  • Whether it is easy to read with your remaining sight: contrast, text size, icons that do not distort or disappear.
  • Whether you find what you are looking for in one or two clicks.
  • Whether the content is clear, useful and realistic.

All questions are optional except those marked as required. We do not ask for your name.
