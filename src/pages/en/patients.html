I have low vision or blindness

Clear information to understand what is happening, what you can do and where to find help, whether you have just been diagnosed or have lived with it for years.

{{icon:bulb}} In a nutshell

  1. Having low vision means you see poorly, even with glasses. It does not mean you see nothing.
  2. Many conditions cannot be cured, but a lot can be done: aids, rehabilitation, technology and support.
  3. A low vision professional helps you choose aids and teaches you how to use them.
  4. It is fine to feel bad. Asking for psychological support early helps.
  5. You are not alone: there are associations and peer support groups.

If you suddenly lose vision

Sudden vision loss, flashes, a curtain-like shadow or severe eye pain are an emergency: go to A&E or call 112. All warning signs.

The content in this area is for guidance and is being reviewed with the clinical team and people with low vision. It does not replace seeing a professional.
