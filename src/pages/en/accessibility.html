Accessibility

This website is designed to be usable by people with different levels of sight and hearing, and with different levels of digital skills.

Our commitment

Our aim is to meet level AA of the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.2 and, wherever possible, level AAA. Accessibility is validated with people with low vision, with advice from the Associació Discapacitat Visual Catalunya: B1+B2+B3 and the Fundació Punt de Vista.

This statement will be updated with the results of user testing.

Tools to adapt the website

At the top of every page there is an always-visible accessibility bar with the most requested options:

  • Text A− / A+: text size from 100% to 300%. Icons grow with the text without distorting, and blocks reflow without overlapping.
  • Contrast: quick switch between a light background (black on white) and a dark background (white on black), in both directions.
  • Listen to this page: a built-in reader that reads the page aloud, designed for public computers or for anyone without a screen reader installed.

More options opens the full panel:

  • seven colour schemes: automatic; light background (light, black on white, black on yellow); dark background (dark, white on black, yellow on black);
  • line spacing and letter and word spacing, each with three levels;
  • highlighted links, reading guide, large cursor and greyscale;
  • stopping animations and motion;
  • read-aloud with pause and adjustable speed.

The guides in the family area also have a Listen to this guide button. Preferences are only saved in your browser. No personal data is collected.

How it is designed

  • Contrast: all text and background combinations have a contrast ratio of at least 7:1.
  • System contrast modes: the website respects Windows, macOS and mobile high-contrast modes without hiding icons, symbols or separators.
  • Typeface: Atkinson Hyperlegible, created by the Braille Institute to make reading easier for people with low vision. The base size is 18 pixels.
  • Zoom: the website adapts to small screens and can be zoomed to 400% without losing content or scrolling horizontally.
  • Keyboard: everything can be done with the keyboard. Focus is highly visible and there is a link to skip straight to the content.
  • Screen readers and braille: landmarks (header, navigation, main content, footer) and sequential h1 to h3 headings without gaps, checked automatically by the publishing system. Tested with VoiceOver, TalkBack, NVDA and braille displays.
  • Images: all informative images have hand-written alternative text, without relying on automatic AI tools.
  • Videos: no video is published without captions, audio description and a full transcript.
  • Two clicks: any section can be reached from the home page in one or two clicks, and the resource assistant goes straight to the content.
  • Not just colour: the three areas are also distinguished by name, number and icon.
  • Language: plain language and “In a nutshell” summaries.

Hearing accessibility

  • All information on the website is available as text.
  • Website notifications (for example, when you change a setting) are shown visually and also announced to screen readers.
  • Videos and audio will have synchronised captions, audio description and a full transcript. Catalan Sign Language (LSC) interpretation will be considered for the main content.

Tips for your device

  • Zoom the page: Ctrl and + (Windows and Linux) or Cmd and + (Mac). To return to normal size: Ctrl and 0.
  • Mobile: in your phone's accessibility settings you can enlarge text and turn on magnification and the screen reader (VoiceOver on iPhone, TalkBack on Android).
  • Reader mode: many browsers have a reader mode that shows only the main text.

Found a barrier?

If any part of this website is not accessible to you, please tell us through the feedback form or contact the University Vision Centre. You will help us improve it.
